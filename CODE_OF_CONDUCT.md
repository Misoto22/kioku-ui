# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to the people you are working with. Assume the person on the other
side is trying to make the project better, and act like it.

## What this means in practice

**Expected:**

- Critique the work, not the person. "This drops focus on Escape" is useful;
  "you clearly did not test this" is not.
- Accept that reviewers, maintainers, and contributors all have limited time,
  and that a slow reply is not a slight.
- Say when you do not know something. A question costs less than a wrong
  assumption that ships.
- Respect that people work in different languages, timezones, and contexts.

**Not accepted:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, commit
  messages, or anywhere else the project is discussed.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or deliberately derailing a review.

## Scope

This applies anywhere the project is represented: issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit history, and any space where someone is acting on behalf
of Kioku UI.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers through a private GitHub advisory or by
contacting [@Misoto22](https://github.com/Misoto22) directly. Reports are
handled privately.

Maintainers who do not follow this document are subject to it in the same way
as anyone else, and a report about a maintainer will be handled by the others.

## Consequences

Depending on what happened, a maintainer may edit or remove the offending
content, ask for a private apology, issue a warning, or block an account from
the repository. Anything beyond a warning is explained to the person it
applies to.
